About Us

The Complaint Management Portal is an initiative dedicated to improving transparency and accountability by connecting citizens and authorities seamlessly.

We aim to simplify the complaint process, encourage citizen participation, and ensure timely responses to public issues — all through the power of technology.

Our Mission

To empower citizens by providing a reliable platform for reporting and tracking community issues.

Our Vision

A transparent and responsive system that strengthens trust between citizens and service providers.
